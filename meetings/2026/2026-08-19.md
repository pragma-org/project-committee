### 4. Voting Mechanism and Quorum

The committee returned to the question of how decisions are carried, raised at a previous meeting and left unresolved. The committee agreed the following, on the reasoning that a narrow split — three in favour against two opposed — would not represent a sound basis for a recommendation to the board:

1. Decisions should aim for unanimity.
2. Where unanimity is not achievable, a supermajority of four of five members carries the decision.
3. Where neither is achievable, no decision is taken and the matter returns for further discussion.

The secretary noted that the board operates on unanimity and that this has worked well in practice, and suggested that members recording a dissenting vote should give their reasons.

> **Resolution:** The Project Committee adopts a decision rule of unanimity where achievable, failing that a supermajority of four of five, failing that no decision.
>
> - In favour: 3
>   - Filip Blagojević
>   - Geoff Watson
>   - Joshua Marchand
> - Against: 0

The secretary will circulate the agreed rule by email to the full committee, including members not present.
