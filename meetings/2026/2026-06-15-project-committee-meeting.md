## Discussions
- We discussed the need for a few clarifications from Tapdano and Filip is drafting an email to them as well as giving them a formal responce of the application.
- We discussed if the IOG Cardano-Init and ContractsLibrary where in a state where it made sense for them to be part of the project committee application process or if it made more sense for the board of pragma to approve them through article 22, 
the discussion leaned towards it being right for them to apply through application process due to such facts that they had garnered a community allready by showing support through funding and there exits github repositories for the project.
We also discussed if it was a good fit in the ecosystem for Pragma to support or if it was in competition or did not forfill a role that was lacking in Cardano, the discussion leaned toward there being
more differences than similarities to relevant other projects such as cardano-up and supernode.
- Blockfrost presented their proposal to also apply. Discussion leaned towards positive with one of an important infrastructure projects on cardano in terms of user adoption and something that could fit within Pragma organisation. 
Critical questions related to how the current reward structure of node operators would work in an open source decentralised environment and if it made sense to continue that or administrate that through pragma or other entities or the protocol itself, 
other was if there was any pending legal issues and the handover process, where it was clarified the handover would be over an 18 month process.
- We discussed that the secretary will be a father again in October and would like to step down from secretary role at that point. Beatrice agreed to be a temporary secretary until we can find a replacement.
